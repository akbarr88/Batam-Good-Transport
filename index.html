FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have questions? Chat with us on WhatsApp

What is included in the transportation package?

Packages include the vehicle, a professional driver, fuel and standard insurance for the chosen duration. Meals, entrance tickets, parking and tolls (where applicable) are typically borne by the customer. Exact inclusions are confirmed at booking.

Do you provide professional drivers?

Yes. Every booking includes an experienced, courteous driver who knows Batam well. Self-drive (car rental without driver) is not offered.

Can I book an airport or ferry transfer?

Absolutely. We provide one-way and round-trip transfers from Hang Nadim Airport and all major ferry terminals: Batam Centre, Harbour Bay and Sekupang.

Do you provide full-day and half-day services?

Yes. Half-day (typically up to 5 hours) and full-day (typically up to 10 hours) charters are available with a flexible itinerary within Batam.

Can I choose my preferred vehicle?

Yes. Browse our fleet and pick the vehicle that fits your group size and luggage. Subject to availability at your travel date.

Do you provide transportation for large groups?

Yes — from 15-seat vans up to 59-seat big buses, ideal for families, tour groups, corporate events and weddings.

How can I make a reservation?

The fastest way is WhatsApp: click any "Book Now" button, fill in your details and send. You can also use the contact form on this page, which opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled.

What payment methods are available?

Payment options (cash, bank transfer, e-wallet) are confirmed with our team at booking. Please confirm your preferred method when reserving.

Can I customize my itinerary?

Yes. Tell us your preferred destinations, pace and duration and we'll tailor a private itinerary just for you.
